Assembly Guide & BOM

  • Print the body (this STL) in opaque filament (black/white PETG recommended for strength).
  • LED Channel: The model is a solid extrusion. For true channel letters, hollow the inside or use a slicer modifier, or print as hollow with 2-3 wall perimeters and leave space for strip. Common is 8-12mm deep channel.
  • WS2812B / SK6812 or COB LED strip (length ≈ sign width + 10%)
  • Controller: Wemos D1 Mini or ESP32 running WLED
  • 5V power supply sized for your pixel count (~0.06A per pixel full white)
  • Optional translucent white PETG face (print thin 0.8-1.2mm and glue on front)
  • Mounting: holes or UniHook style for van/wall
