Estimated width: ~420 mm

Power @ full white: ~2.2 A @ 5V

Bill of Materials

  • WS2812B or COB LED strip (length calculated above)
  • Wemos D1 Mini or ESP32 + WLED firmware
  • 5V power supply (adequate amps)
  • USB cable or barrel jack
  • Optional: M3 heat inserts + screws for modular assembly
  • Translucent white PETG/PLA for face / diffuser
